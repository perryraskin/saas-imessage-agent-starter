# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the contact options on [Perry Raskin's GitHub profile](https://github.com/perryraskin). All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
